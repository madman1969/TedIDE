TED 7360/8360 - Text Editing Device (jokingly "Tramiels Exit Device") was a chip made by MOS Technology Inc and was the primary chip used in the Commodore Plus/4, C16 and C116. The chip provided video, sound generation hardware, DRAM refresh circuitry, interval timers and keyboard input handling.

Technical Details[edit | edit source]

Video Specifications[edit | edit source]

  • Text mode: 40X25
  • Hi-Res graphics: 320X200
  • Multicolor Graphics Mode: 160X200

Audio Specification[edit | edit source]

  • 2 channel tone generator
    • Channel 1: Square wave
    • Channel 2: Square wave or white noise

7360 vs 8360[edit | edit source]

The 8360 is functionally the same than the 7360 but they are manufactured with a different technology. Most machines contain a 8360.

  • 7360 HMOS-1
  • 8360 HMOS-2
